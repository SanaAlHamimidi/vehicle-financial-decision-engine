Analysis Methods

How We Analyze

The statistical and computational techniques used to surface depreciation patterns, model total cost of ownership, and rank vehicles by financial risk.

Approach

Our analytical pipeline

Step 01

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Handling missing values, outlier removal, normalization of price and mileage, and geographic clustering of California regions.

Step 02

Depreciation Curve Fitting

Using historical price-over-mileage data to fit exponential decay curves per vehicle segment and region. Value retention ratio computed relative to original MSRP.

Step 03

Feature Engineering

Constructing composite features: total cost of ownership score, regional demand index, reliability-weighted depreciation, and interest-adjusted purchase cost.

Step 04

Comparative Analysis

Side-by-side cost trajectories for different vehicles — comparing depreciation, insurance, and interest over a 5-year ownership window.

Rating System

The 5-star depreciation rating

Each vehicle receives a depreciation rating from 1 to 5 stars, derived from:

Factor A

Historical Value Retention

How much of the original MSRP is retained over a defined period — normalized across vehicle class.

Factor B

Reliability Score

Third-party reliability data weighted alongside resale trends to adjust for known mechanical risk.

Factor C

Regional Market Demand

Local supply/demand ratio within California — a truck in rural areas depreciates differently than in urban SF.
