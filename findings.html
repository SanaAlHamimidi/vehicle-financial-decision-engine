Findings

What we found

Finding 01

Region Matters More Than Expected

Trucks in rural Northern California retain significantly more value than identical models listed in the Bay Area — up to 18% difference in value retention over 3 years.

Finding 02

Mileage Threshold Effect

Depreciation accelerates sharply past certain mileage thresholds (e.g., 100k miles). Buyers and sellers often miss this inflection point in negotiation.

Finding 03

Luxury ≠ Better Value

Several luxury segments depreciate 30–40% faster in the first two years compared to mid-range reliable models, driven by high initial price and maintenance costs.

Finding 04

Total Cost Often 2× Sticker

When interest, insurance, and depreciation loss are combined over a 5-year period, total cost of ownership frequently exceeds 2× the purchase price for lower-rated vehicles.
