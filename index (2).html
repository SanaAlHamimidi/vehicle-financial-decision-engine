Project Summary

Beyond the sticker price

One of the biggest purchases individuals make after buying a home is a car. While the listed price is a reasonable metric, an often overlooked aspect is the long-term financial ripple hidden at the time of purchase.

Our platform surfaces depreciation, insurance costs, and interest rates — letting buyers make truly informed decisions before signing anything.

Vehicle depreciation doesn't behave uniformly across regions. A truck may hold its value well in rural Northern California while depreciating faster in dense urban markets like San Francisco. Our platform lets users visually compare depreciation curves across regions.
