Understanding the Data

A deep dive into the distributions, patterns, and relationships across vehicle listings, depreciation rates, insurance costs, and regional market data.

Dataset Overview

What we're working with

Dataset 01

Used Vehicle Listings

Craigslist-sourced dataset with price, mileage, make, model, year, and condition. Covers thousands of listings across California.

Dataset 02

Insurance Expenditure

NAIC average expenditure by state (2019–2023). Used to model regional insurance cost variation as a function of vehicle value.

Dataset 03

Regional Demand Signals

Vehicle registration and sales data to capture market demand by region — a key weight in the depreciation rating model.
