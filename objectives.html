Project Objectives
Goals, research questions, and data sources

🎯 Motivation

The decision to purchase a vehicle is one of the most significant financial commitments an individual makes — yet most buyers lack access to tools that transparently quantify the long-term financial implications of that decision. This project was motivated by a personal experience of purchasing a car without fully understanding the financial ripple effect of vehicle depreciation.

Beyond individual depreciation, California's used car market is undergoing a structural shift driven by the rapid adoption of electric vehicles. A gasoline vehicle purchased today is entering a market that is increasingly moving away from it — a dynamic that existing depreciation calculators do not adequately capture.

This project aims to build a data-driven tool that helps California vehicle owners make more informed financial decisions about buying, holding, and selling their vehicles.

🔍 Research Questions

Three key research questions guide this analysis:

01
Which vehicle features affect resale price the most?

We investigate which characteristics — age, mileage, condition, fuel type, brand, and vehicle type — are the strongest predictors of used car resale price in the California market.

02
How does geography in California affect resale value?

We examine whether location independently influences resale value beyond vehicle characteristics, and whether regional demand — captured from DMV registration data — provides a meaningful signal for depreciation rates.

03
Does selling the same vehicle across California regions yield different resale prices?

We test whether the same vehicle with identical features commands meaningfully different prices in different California regions, and identify which regions offer the highest potential return for sellers.

📦 Data Sources

This project combines 8 datasets totaling over 3.6 million records across three categories:

Vehicle Listings

vehicles.csv

Craigslist used car listings scraped across the US. Filtered to 50,614 California listings with 26 features including price, year, manufacturer, model, condition, fuel type, odometer, and region.

Registration Data

vehicle-fuel-type-count CSVs (×6)

California DMV vehicle registration data from 2019 to 2024, broken down by ZIP code, fuel type, and make. Combined into 3.6 million rows. Used to engineer the regional demand feature.

MSRP Reference

car_data.csv

Vehicle MSRP values across multiple brands and years. Used as the original price reference for calculating depreciation rates. Contains approximately 100,000 listings across 13 brands.

Target Variable & Features of Interest

Target Variable:

Price — the current resale listing price of a used vehicle in California

Key Features:

Year, odometer, manufacturer, condition, fuel type, vehicle type, region, engineered demand score

🗺️ Project Scope

In Scope

  • California used car market only
  • Personal use vehicles (not commercial)
  • Vehicles manufactured from 2000 onwards
  • Price range $500 to $150,000
  • 29 California regions covered
  • 6 years of registration data (2019–2024)

Out of Scope

  • Dealer or auction market pricing
  • Commercial or fleet vehicles
  • Vehicles outside California
  • Real-time market data
  • Insurance or maintenance cost modeling
